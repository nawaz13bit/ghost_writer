New Project

Length picks defaults below - override any of them to set your own total length, chapter count, or pacing per chapter. Leave a field blank to keep the preset's value.

Import Manuscript

Pick .docx/.md/.txt files (or a whole folder). Files can mix finished prose, outline notes, character/world notes, and freeform thoughts - each is sorted automatically into chapters/outline/characters/world if the LLM server is running, or filed as an unsorted note for you to sort if it isn't. Using the same title as an existing project adds to it instead of creating a new one. Only premise is required - title/genre are auto-suggested if left blank and can be edited later.

New Idea

A chapter idea you haven't placed yet - no act or chapter # needed. Once you know where it fits, promote it into the outline.

Integrate Idea into Chapter

Pick a drafted chapter for the AI to revise so it weaves this idea in. Shows as a normal revision diff - nothing is saved until you approve it. Once approved, the idea is marked resolved and drops off the open backlog.

New Outline Entry

Adds a chapter to the outline. Typing an Act that doesn't exist yet creates it - acts are just a label, not a separate list. Set the chapter # first, then either describe the chapter and let AI draft the title/act/summary, or fill them in yourself.

Revise Whole Outline

Describe how the whole plot outline should change - add/remove/reorder chapters, shift a subplot, compress an act, whatever. Chapters that already have drafted or approved prose are protected: the AI is told to keep their chapter number and not contradict what's already written.

Review and edit before applying - nothing is saved until you click Apply. One chapter per line: chapter # | act | title | summary | setup | event | resolution. Trailing fields (setup/event/resolution) may be left blank. Add or delete lines to add/remove chapters; blank lines are ignored.

Add Project to Series

Renames this project's folder to be namespaced under the series and creates/updates a shared series bible seeded with this project's current characters, world entries, and notes. Future books in the same series will inherit that canon and a recap of this book. This cannot be undone from the UI - back up the project folder first if unsure.
